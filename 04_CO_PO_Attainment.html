Source boundary

The supplied syllabus provides five COs and experiment-wise CO alignment, plus a CIE/SEE rubric with CO–PO mapping. It does not provide a complete numeric CO–PO matrix, PSOs, attainment targets, direct/indirect weightage, or university-approved attainment formula.

This phase therefore separates source-derived mappings from editable institutional attainment settings. It does not present invented values as official VTU/NBA values.

Recommended evidence flow

  1. Phase 1: CO and experiment structure.
  2. Phase 2: teaching and attendance evidence.
  3. Phase 3: CIE/SEE performance.
  4. Phase 4: attainment calculation and justification.
  5. Phase 5: consolidated NBA-ready evidence reports.
